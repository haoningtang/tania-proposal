# T16a: hex E1 line, TE/TM band curvature over (t2 − t, r2 − r)
- Run: `inverse/curv_map.py` (7 × 7 grid, −40…+40 nm in 13.33 nm steps; base a 850, r 212.5, t 280 nm, gap 380, the T12a line at 1498.56 nm, Q 133). Settings: 80/96, θ 0.5/3/6°, azimuth 0. The line is followed between grid points in 4 substeps at 0.5°, with one shared TE/TM scan. GPU took rows 0, +13, +27 and the CPU took −13, −27, −40, +40. Analysis: `inverse/curv_map_analyze.py` → analysis.json/png, table.csv. `v1_coarse_steps/` (no substeps) is superseded: it hopped to another line at r2 − r = +13.
- **Not met: c_TM never reaches 0.** On the 45 bright points (Q 51–491), c_TM runs from −0.507 to −0.119 (M_TM 0.99–4.2), and nowhere is |M| ≥ 10 for both polarizations. c_TE runs from −0.110 to +0.137, so it changes sign. Closest approach: (t2 − t, r2 − r) = (−40, 0) nm, λ 1477.1 nm, Q 97, M_TE −6.0, M_TM +4.2. The largest M_TE is +13.7 at (+13, 0), Q 491, where M_TM is 1.46.
- **Caveat on line identity.** The λ map has two plateaus, ≈ 1500–1519 nm (c_TE < 0, M_TE ≈ +5) and ≈ 1469–1477 nm (c_TE > 0, M_TE ≈ −4), and the c_TE = 0 contour sits mostly on the boundary between them. So that contour marks a change of line (or an anticrossing) more than a smooth zero of one band. 17/49 points have a 0.5° λ step larger than half a linewidth along the follow path (dots in the figure). The 4 points at t2 − t = −27 nm, r2 − r ≤ 0 land on Q ~ 1e5 quasi-BIC lines (masked). One of them has M_TM +70 but M_TE −3.3.
- Meaning: breaking the up/down symmetry over ±40 nm does not flatten TM on the bright lines here. |c_TM| falls from ≈ 0.45 to ≈ 0.12–0.19 on the ≈ 1471 nm plateau (larger r2, or t2 − t = −40) and then levels off. Not checked: 200/192, azimuth 30°, tuning (not run, since there is no crossing point), and larger offsets.
