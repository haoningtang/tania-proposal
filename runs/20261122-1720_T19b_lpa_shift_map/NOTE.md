# T19b: LPA reference, aligned square bilayer with uniform shift δ (fmmax 80/96)
- 16×16 shifts over one cell (45 C4v-distinct computed, rest by symmetry), normal incidence, T19a λ grid (70 values, 1480–1580 nm). Complex 2×2 Jones in `lpa_jones.npz` (J[i, j, λ, out, in], x/y). Aligned line: T_x min 0.002 at 1508.0 nm (1 nm grid point; T15 had 1506.5–1508).
- Checks: J_xy = J_yx to 6e-9 at every shift (inversion + reciprocity); CD is zero at δ = 0 (1e-12) and on all four mirror lines (≤ 2e-8); CD(−δx, δy) = −CD(δx, δy) (residual 7e-8); shift-averaged CD ≤ 6e-15.
- **CD_LPA is not zero at generic shifts:** |CD| up to 0.24 (δ = (4, 5)a/16, 1524 nm); 0.03–0.08 at many off-mirror shifts. It equals 2 Im[(J_xx − J_yy) J_xy*] exactly (residual 1e-8): a symmetric Jones matrix with a linear eigenbasis not along x/y (linear dichroism + birefringence on rotated axes) gives T_σ+ ≠ T_σ− without chirality. So "every stacking is inversion-symmetric ⇒ CD = 0" holds only on the mirror lines and for the cell average.
- Meaning for T19c: CD_LPA(δ) transforms as A2 of C4v (odd under all mirrors), so near AA it starts as δxδy(δx² − δy²), i.e. fourth order in θ·r, and it averages to 0 over any C4-symmetric aperture centred at AA. The local map of T19c must be compared with this nonzero LPA map, not with 0; the gradient (beyond-LPA) part is T19a − LPA.
- Not checked: higher resolution than 80/96; finer shift grid.
